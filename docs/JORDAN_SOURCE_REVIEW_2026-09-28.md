## Implications

Manual sourcing adds relevant evidence absent from the tested API queries. It does not establish API-wide absence: the WUDUH advert predates that query window. Three shortlisted pages are not three validated new database rows; identity/deduplication, permitted reuse, exact dates and inclusion rules still need review.

A historical requirement snapshot is useful for this MVP if visibly dated and not described as live hiring demand. Do not discard valid junior roles solely because they allow 1–2 years; Junior Data Analyst and fresh-graduate-only are different population definitions. Decide and document the inclusion rule before recalculating requirements.

Inspection of existing source data also found older dates and mixed levels/titles, including a CRM Supervisor, a Data Associate requiring 3+ years and a Business Analyst. Audit existing real entries along with prospective additions. Source labeling alone does not establish suitability.

Recommendation: use a small transparent, dated Jordan junior/early-career analyst snapshot; prioritize the Zain and WUDUH employer pages, review the anonymous graduate ad separately, and retain clear historical/adjacent-role labels. No purchase of another API justified by this pass. Not enough evidence for 30–50 distinct suitable postings or a representative market estimate. Scoring, role scope and database unchanged.
